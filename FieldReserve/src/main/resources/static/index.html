Welcome to Field Booking Application

Our idea is to build a digital field booking system called FieldReserve, a web-based app that lets sports enthusiasts and community groups browse available sports fields and submit booking requests easily without conflicts. Instead of complicated scheduling platforms or calling to reserve, FieldReserve focuses on simple reservations, clear availability, and persistent user selections. Users pick a field and date/time, the system automatically checks availability based on existing bookings, prevents basic conflicts, and then sends a confirmation email with the booking details to make community field reservations safer, faster, and more organized.
